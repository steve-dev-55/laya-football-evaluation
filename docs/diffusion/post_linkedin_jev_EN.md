You know Jev? Meet Laya — the open-source version of Jev.

Same playing field: football, probabilities, real-time decisions. One fundamental difference: here, everything is verifiable.

**Scientific preregistration** — hypotheses, metrics and decision thresholds frozen and published BEFORE any testing (DOI: 10.17605/OSF.IO/TPSQB). No rewriting history.

**100% open code (MIT)** — a 9-agent pipeline, from data collection to the final report, auditable line by line.

**Automatic leakage prevention** — the AI never sees information that did not exist at prediction time. Verified on 100% of snapshots.

**Honest baselines** — Poisson, Dixon–Coles, bookmaker odds… trained on exactly the same information as the AI, at the same moments.

A closed prediction engine asks you to take its word for it. Laya asks you to verify.

The evaluation starts on 2,403 matches (10 competitions, 3 complete seasons). The results will be what they will be — that is precisely the point of a preregistered study.

Code: https://github.com/steve-dev-55/laya-football-evaluation
Protocol: https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/TPSQB
